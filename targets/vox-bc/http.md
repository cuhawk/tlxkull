## Focus Areas

- Payment cycle: book tickets / buy items without payment
- Access to other users' tickets/orders
- Remotely gain access to other users' PCI details (credit card, CVV)
- Remotely gain access to other users' personal details (name, contact, passwords)
- Unauthorized changes/manipulation of coupons, vouchers, loyalty points
- Exfiltration of personal or credit card data
- Remote unauthorized DB access
- Remote code execution
- Defacement

## Out-of-Scope

- Modifying data in accounts not belonging to you
- Downloading data beyond 1-2 records minimum
- DoS/DDoS or volumetric attacks
- Third-party apps/services integrating with the system
- DMARC issues
- Credential stuffing / leaked credential dumps
- Credential leak reports (appreciated but not eligible for reward)
- Zero-day vulns: allow 15 business days after patch before submission
- http://www.theplaymania.com/ (removed from scope Jul 2021)
