Hi there, I'd actually you a greatly feature we just added to Burpsuite Pro and Community
Edition.
So what you can now do from then Burp is to launch Burps in Beds Browser, pre-configured
and ready to work with Burps proxy.
In the past you used to have to go into your browser settings and configure the address
of the proxy listener, you then had to import Burps the AC to get rid of TLS warnings.
Now, you don't have to do any of that, you just go to Burp Proxy, click the button and the
browser opens ready to go.
Even if you've got your proxy listener settings messed up, if you don't have one or if
it is not turned on or if it can't bind to the configured port, don't worry, but we'll
just figure things out and make it work.
If you want to still configure things the old way, if you want to test on a different
browser or a set client, maybe on a mobile device, all of the old way still works just
the same as before.
Anyway, hope you have fun and enjoy, as always, do let us have any feedback.
Thank you.
