We're going to take a look now at how to manage certificates for outbound connections from your
Burp Suite Enterprise server. Certificates for inbound traffic, for example HTTPS to the
Enterprise web server, are managed separately. You can add or remove certificates so that Burp
Suite Enterprise knows which external systems to trust. For example, certificates used for Jira or
email servers. You can upload certificates for an internal certificate authority or individually
self-signed certs. You should only upload certificates from trusted sources. Once you have logged in as an
administrator, you can go to the settings menu and choose network. Scroll down to manage certificates.
To add a certificate, click on upload certificate. Your certificate should be in PEM format.
To remove certificates, you can click on the small cross next to the certificate.
