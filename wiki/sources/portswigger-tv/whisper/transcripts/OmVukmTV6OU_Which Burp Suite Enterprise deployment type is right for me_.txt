When deploying Burp Suite Enterprise, you can choose between deploying via our standard
installer or our Kubernetes Helm chart. The standard deployment is typically used for
smaller deployments where your scanning requirements are known and predictable.
In contrast, the Kubernetes deployment is typically used for larger deployments where
the scanning requirements vary and you would benefit from the auto-scaling infrastructure.
If you have no Kubernetes experience, we would advise using the standard installer.
There is also a quick start option which utilizes the standard installer with our default options
and bundle database. This is suitable if you're looking to trial Burp Suite Enterprise.
The downloads can be accessed from this page, which will direct you to the respective release notes.
