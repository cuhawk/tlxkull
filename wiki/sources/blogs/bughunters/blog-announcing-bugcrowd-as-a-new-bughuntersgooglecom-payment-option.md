# Announcing Bugcrowd as a new bughunters.google.com payment option

We've heard your feedback loud and clear: the payment process on
bughunters.google.com had room for improvement. Slow payments and limited
options made getting rewarded for your hard work a sometimes frustrating
experience.

That's why we're thrilled to announce a major upgrade: Bugcrowd is now live as a
new payment option!

### What This Means for You

We're committed to making bug hunting on our platform a rewarding and enjoyable
experience. By partnering with Bugcrowd, we're taking a big step towards that
goal: Bugcrowd makes it easier and faster to get paid and they offer various
methods for you to receive your monetary rewards. This means you'll spend less
time worrying about payments and more time doing what you do best – uncovering
vulnerabilities and helping us improve the security of the Internet.

### How to Get Started

If you're already a registered bug hunter on bughunters.google.com, switching to
Bugcrowd is easy: Just update your payment preferences in your profile settings
to “Bugcrowd” and enter the email address you use with Bugcrowd. Your new
settings will apply to all future rewards.

![](https://storage.googleapis.com/bughunters-article-images/blogs/bugcrowd_01.png)

_Fig. 1. The "Payment Options" section of the Edit Profile dialog_

Not yet registered with Bugcrowd? See [https://www.bugcrowd.com/](https://www.bugcrowd.com/) for information
on registering and how to receive payments via their platform.

### What’s Next

We're hard at work to be able to offer you additional payment options, stay
tuned for more details! :)
