# ESCAL8 de Google: ¡Hacking, Héroes y Horizontes en la Ciudad de México! (ESCAL8 is coming to Mexico!)

Besides ensuring that Google's systems and users are protected from harm, our
Security Engineering teams are passionate about three things: fostering
brilliant new talent for the future of the industry, sharing knowledge gained
from years of tackling complex security problems, and promoting team hacking
through Capture the Flag (CTF) competitions. These core interests converge
annually at our **ESCAL8 conference**, an event that has grown from a small CTF
in a Zurich meeting room to a gathering of over one hundred invited attendees,
rotating locations each year.

In this blog post, we’ll take a look at what happened at ESCAL8 2024 in Málaga,
Spain, as well as shining the spotlight on what we have planned for ESCAL8 2025,
which we’re hosting in October in Mexico City.

## ESCAL8 2024: A Convergence of Cyber Expertise in Málaga

Last October, ESCAL8 took place **in Málaga, Spain**, at the
[Google Safety Engineering Center in Málaga](https://safety.google/engineering-center-malaga/?_gl=1*1o1g7ft*_up*MQ..*_ga*MTMzMTk4ODAxMC4xNzU3MDcyMzA2*_ga_66DHBKG664*czE3NTcwNzIzMDYkbzEkZzAkdDE3NTcwNzIzMDYkajYwJGwwJGgw)
and the Málaga University of Computer Science and Engineering. This edition
brought together **321 security researchers, CTF players, students, key opinion**
**formers, and Googlers** to learn, share knowledge, and engage in hacking
activities.

Highlights from ESCAL8 2024 include:

- **init.g(málaga) – Student Workshop:** This initiative offered a variety of
workshops and talks to **encourage over 50 students from underrepresented**
**groups**—including 50% women—to pursue careers in cybersecurity.
init.g(students) attended sessions on topics like offensive security, web
security, cryptography or AI security. This workshop has expanded beyond
ESCAL8; last year we also engaged with student communities in Sao Paulo,
Paris, and Las Vegas. Check out our
[website](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/buildyourfuture/events/init.g-cybersecurityworkshopforstudents)
if you are curious to learn what’s on our agenda this year!
- **Hackceler8 – The Google CTF Finals:** Recognized as one of the world's
highest-rated competitions, Hackceler8 delivered an **exciting, fast-paced,**
**and challenging e-sports-like event** for the world’s best CTF teams. The
finals were livestreamed on YouTube, attracting 5.2k views.
- **bugSWAT – Live Hacking Event:** This three-day event brought together **40**
**of our [top bug hunters](https://bughunters.google.com/leaderboard)** from
over 17 countries for training, knowledge-sharing, and live-hacking on 16
Google products. This intense session resulted in **40 bug reports** with
total rewards exceeding $170,000.
- **Safer with Google KOF Workshop:** [Google Safety Engineering Center in Málaga](https://safety.google/engineering-center-malaga/?_gl=1*1o1g7ft*_up*MQ..*_ga*MTMzMTk4ODAxMC4xNzU3MDcyMzA2*_ga_66DHBKG664*czE3NTcwNzIzMDYkbzEkZzAkdDE3NTcwNzIzMDYkajYwJGwwJGgw),
engaged with **35 Spanish key opinion formers** from public policy, private
industry, and academia. Discussions focused on Google’s approach to cyber
secure AI systems, threat intelligence, vulnerability research, and its
commitment to cybersecurity in Spain.
- **Want to know more?** Check out our video recap at the end of this blog
post!

## What’s Next? ESCAL8 2025 in Mexico City!

This October, for the first time in its history, ESCAL8 2025 is set to take
place in Mexico City, with the following highlights:

- **init.g(mexico\_city) – Student Workshop:** Designed to inspire students and
promote diversity in cybersecurity, this workshop will run for **two days**.
The program aims to reach a broad group of **~60 students** from Mexico
City. Selected participants will learn the basics of hardware hacking, web
security, cryptography, and red teaming, with plenty of time for networking
with Googlers and experts in the field.
- **Hackceler8 – Google CTF Finals:** This year's Capture the Flag finals will
again bring together the
**[top 8 teams from the qualification round](https://capturetheflag.withgoogle.com/scoreboard/)**
to compete in an exciting, esports-like hacking competition. Teams will race
against time speedrunning videogame hacking challenges on an old-school
console, finding security exploits and facing against new and terrifying
foes.
- **bugSWAT – Live Hacking Event:** Our annual bug hunter event for our top
researchers. This event will feature special scope/bonuses and close
collaboration with many of our VRPs such as Google, Android, Abuse, Chrome,
Cloud, OSS, and Mobile. We're already looking forward to finding an answer
to the question: Who will win the "most valuable hacker" at our premiere
event!?
- **Safer with Google KOF Workshops:** Google's cybersecurity thought
leadership will be shared through talks and workshops for **government**
**officials and key opinion formers**.

We look forward to another impactful edition of ESCAL8, and are delighted to be
hosting this event in Mexico City for the first time in history, continuing
Google's commitment to advancing cybersecurity through community engagement,
knowledge sharing, and talent development.

Hack on! And be sure to follow us on [x.com/googleVRP](https://x.com/googleVRP)
for updates.
