# AI bugSWAT in Tokyo & 2025 Hacker Roadshow

We care deeply about the quality of our VRP programs and continuously invest in
them to ensure that Google and Alphabet products remain safe for our users. In
this blog post, we want to present one of the related events we host regularly
to complement our VRP program – bugSWAT!

### Interested in the premiere live hacking event on the planet?

Since 2017, we have run regular bugSWAT events where our top bughunters are
invited to gather in order to learn and pen-test/hack Google products alongside
Googlers.

The combination of having talented security researchers together in a room with
Google security engineers has led to the discovery of many impactful bugs in the
past. To maximize this effect, this year, we are expanding this effort to span
four global events!

### What makes Google live hacking events special?

- Direct access to Googlers: During live hacking events, we pull in subject
matter and security experts for our products. We collaborate with individual
bughunters to help them demonstrate the impact of their reports and thus
maximize their potential rewards.
- Special access: We routinely provide researchers with early access enabling
them to hack unreleased products and features.
- Special bonuses: In order to keep our events exceptional, we provide
product-specific and collaboration bonuses, which can increase the standard
rewards communicated in our
[program rules](https://bughunters.google.com/about/rules/6744710187712512)
by up to 200%!
- Travel is on us: We reimburse flight/hotel/food expenses according to our
established guidelines and policies.

### What just happened in Tokyo?

In April 2025, we invited 14 of our top AI bughunters to participate in 2 days
of training, knowledge-sharing, and hacking on a variety of Google AI products.
The participation was exhilarating with bughunters submitting numerous reports,
which resulted in a (preliminary) reward total of **$359,400**.

### I'd love to join future hacking events, how do I get invited?

Google typically considers the performance of individual researchers from the
past year of VRP bug reports as well as previous bugSWAT event performance when
deciding who to invite.

Remember, this is about quality, not quantity – the higher the impact of your
reports is, the more you'll be rewarded, and the higher you'll move up in the
ranking of impactful researchers, increasing your chances of receiving an
invitation to bugSWAT.

### What’s next for 2025 and beyond?

In the second half of 2025, Google is continuing the hacker roadshow and is
planning to host multiple bugSWAT events in Sunnyvale, Las Vegas and Mexico
City! If you have been selected to participate in one of these events, you'll
soon find an invitation email in your inbox providing further details.
