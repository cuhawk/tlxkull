# Time to challenge yourself in the 2024 Google CTF

It’s Google CTF time! Install your tools, commit your scripts, and clear your
schedule. The competition kicks off on June 21 2024 6:00 PM UTC and runs through
June 23 2024 6:00 PM UTC. Registration is now open at
[goo.gle/ctf](https://goo.gle/ctf).

![](https://storage.googleapis.com/bughunters-article-images/blogs/ctf_2024_04.png)

_Fig. 1. The Google CTF logo_

Join the Google CTF (at [goo.gle/ctf](https://goo.gle/ctf)), a thrilling arena
to showcase your technical prowess. The Google CTF consists of a set of computer
security puzzles (or challenges) involving reverse-engineering, memory
corruption, cryptography, web technologies, and more. Participants can use
obscure security knowledge to find exploits through bugs and creative misuse,
and with each completed challenge your team will earn points and move up through
the ranks.

The top 8 teams of the Google CTF will qualify for our
[Hackceler8](https://capturetheflag.withgoogle.com/hackceler8#about) competition
taking place in Málaga, Spain later this year as a part of our larger
[Escal8 event](https://bughunters.google.com/blog/4846227460325376). Hackceler8
is our experimental esport-style hacking game competition, custom-made to mix
CTF and speedrunning.

![](https://storage.googleapis.com/bughunters-article-images/blogs/ctf_2024_02.png)

_Fig. 2. Screenshot from last year’s Hackceler8 game_

In the competition, teams need to find clever ways to abuse the game features to
capture flags as quickly as possible.

Last year, teams assumed the role of Bartholomew (Mew for short), the fuzzy and
adorable protagonist of Hackceler8 2023, set to defeat and overcome the evil
rA.Ibbit taking over Silicon Valley! What adventures will Mew encounter this
year? See
[the 2023 grand final](https://www.youtube.com/live/HFeD4kYcW7A?si=uwFfZ0Y9-YnbC2nh&t=331)
to get a sense of the story and gameplay. The prize pool for this year’s Google
CTF and Hackceler8 stands at more than $32,000.

![](https://storage.googleapis.com/bughunters-article-images/blogs/ctf_2024_05.png)

_Fig. 3. The Hackceler8 logo_

Itching to get started early? Want to learn more, or get a leg up on the
competition? Review challenges from previous years, including previous
Hackceler8 matches, all open-sourced
[here](https://github.com/google/google-ctf). Or gain inspiration by binge
watching
[hours of Hackceler8 2023 videos](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=hackceler8)!

If you are just starting out in this space, check out our documentary
[H4CK1NG GOOGLE](https://h4ck1ng.google/), it’s a great way to get acquainted
with security. We also recommend checking out this year’s
[Beginner’s Quest](https://capturetheflag.withgoogle.com/beginners-quest)
that’ll be launching later this summer which will teach you some of the tools
and tricks with simpler gamified challenges. For example, last year we explored
[hacking through time](https://gctf-2023.uc.r.appspot.com/) – you can use this
to prepare for what’s yet to come.

Whether you're a seasoned CTF player or just curious about cybersecurity and
ethical hacking, we want to invite you to join us. Sign up for the Google CTF to
expand your skill set, meet new friends in the security community, and even
watch the pros in action. For the latest announcements, see
[goo.gle/ctf](https://goo.gle/ctf),
[subscribe to our mailing list](https://groups.google.com/forum/?nomobile=true#!forum/google-ctf/join),
or follow us on Twitter [@GoogleVRP](http://twitter.com/googlevrp). Interested
in bug hunting for Google? Check out
[bughunters.google.com](http://bughunters.google.com/). See you there!
