# ESCAL8 2025: Gathering Cybersecurity Expertise in Mexico City

Held from October 2-5, Google's annual security conference, ESCAL8, touched down
in México City this year. The conference brought together nearly 200 attendees
from across the globe, including security researchers, CTF players, students,
Key Opinion Formers (KOFs), and Googlers.

As always, ESCAL8 was focused on collaboration, knowledge-sharing, and a
commitment to a safer digital world. In the following, let’s take a closer look
at the four main segments of this year’s edition of ESCAL8.

Tip: For a video summary of ESCAL8 2025 highlights, scroll to the end of this
post.

## bugSWAT: Hacking for a Safer Internet

bugSWAT, together with
[our VRPs](https://bughunters.google.com/about/rules/6744710187712512),
showcases an important cornerstone of Google's proactive approach to securing
its products: collaboration with external researchers. Over the years, direct
dialogue with the security community has proved invaluable for learning new
exploitation techniques and strengthening product security for the benefit of
our users.

The final 2025 edition of bugSWAT brought together 38 of the world's top bug
hunters for three days of intensive training, knowledge exchange, and
live-hacking across many different targets and spaces including AI, Android, and
Cloud.

> Did you know? As part of ESCAL8 2025, we rolled out our new
> [AI Vulnerability Reward Program](https://bughunters.google.com/blog/6116887259840512/announcing-google-s-new-ai-vulnerability-reward-program)
> to double down on our commitment to provide our users with a secure AI user
> experience, and reward security researchers who invest their time and effort
> to discover and report AI-related vulnerabilities.

## Hackceler8: The Google CTF Final

The Google CTF Final, better known as "Hackceler8", solidified its position as
one of the world's highest-rated competitions. The top 8 teams from the online
qualification round (from over 250 teams) competed in an exciting, fast-paced,
and gamified "e-sport" style final stage. Teams raced to solve
visually-designed, security-based tasks for prizes and recognition.

Big congratulations to [Kalmarunionen](https://twitter.com/kalmarunionenDM), who
took the win, and kudos to [Zer0RocketWrecks](https://ctftime.org/team/307823)
for second place! Finally, our semi-finalists
[DiceGang](https://twitter.com/dicegangctf) and
[Maple Mallard Magistrates](https://twitter.com/mmm_ctf_team) also deserve a big
shout out.

## init.g(mexico): Inspiring the Next Generation of Security Heroes

Over 60 students from local universities came together to participate in
**init.g(mexico)**, our two-day intro-level cybersecurity workshop. Over these
two days, students joined a variety of workshops and talks on topics like
offensive security, web security, and cryptography. All sessions were
specifically designed to **inspire students from varied groups** to pursue
careers in cybersecurity.

What can we say? The students were incredibly engaged and praised various
aspects of the event in their feedback:

- _The conferences were really interesting and were a great introduction for_
_so many topics._
- _Attending Init.g()_ \[...\] _gave me a lot of direction for my career and a_
_much better idea of what I want to do._
- _It was an incredibly enriching learning experience, diving deep into_
_various facets of Cybersecurity._
- _It was an incredible experience full of learning, challenges, and inspiring_
_talks about the future of this fascinating field._
- _I left feeling motivated and excited to keep exploring the world of_
_cybersecurity._

And, along the way, init.g(mexico) also helped us strengthen the relationship
between Google and Mexican universities. We look forward to seeing many of the
workshop participants join the security industry in the coming years!

## Safer with Google & KOF Engagement

As the final element of ESCAL8, Google Security (GSEC) hosted a **Safer with**
**Google** seminar, sharing technical thought leadership with Mexican government
officials. This was complemented by a successful Key Opinion Former (KOF) event
showcasing Google's expertise in AI Cybersecurity, which brought together 24
influential figures from public policy, private industry, and academia, with a
focus on national cyber resilience strategies and the future of secure AI
systems. In addition, attendees received a guided tour of ESCAL8, where they
witnessed live-hacking demonstrations, providing a unique insight into the
external security community's work and contributions.

## Conclusion

ESCAL8 2025 was more than just a conference; it demonstrated the power of
community engagement, providing an essential platform for knowledge exchange,
fostering new talent, and reiterating Google’s leadership in the cybersecurity
industry. We look forward to building on this success and continuing our
commitment to a safer digital future through future editions of ESCAL8.

See you there!
