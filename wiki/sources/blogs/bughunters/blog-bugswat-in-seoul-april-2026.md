# bugSWAT in Seoul – April 2026

![](https://storage.googleapis.com/bughunters-article-images/blogs/bugswat_2026.png)

At Google, we continuously invest in the quality of our VRP programs and our
relations with the external security researcher / bug hunter community – all
with the goal of ensuring that Google and Alphabet products maintain the highest
possible security standards and remain safe for our users. In this blog post,
you can find details on our latest edition of **bugSWAT**, our premier
live-hacking event!

## What are bugSWAT events about?

Since 2017, Google has been running regular bugSWAT events to complement our VRP
program. The goal of these events is to bring our top bug hunters together with
Googlers at a physical location (usually a Google office), enabling them to
learn from each other, network, and join forces to probe the security defenses
of selected Google products.

But what exactly makes these live-hacking events so special? The most important
factors are:

- Direct access to Googlers: During live hacking events, we pull in subject
matter and security experts for our products. We collaborate with individual
bug hunters to help them demonstrate the impact of their reports and thus
maximize their potential rewards. The combination of having talented
security researchers together in a room with Google security engineers has
led to the discovery of many impactful bugs.
- Special access: We routinely provide researchers with early access enabling
them to hack unreleased products and features.
- Special bonuses: In order to keep our events exceptional, we provide
product-specific and collaboration bonuses, which can increase the standard
rewards communicated in our program rules by up to 100% more!
- Travel is on us: We reimburse flight/hotel/food expenses according to our
established guidelines and policies.

**Would you like to participate in a future event?**

bugSWAT is an invitation-only event. We invite researchers based on clear
criteria, such as the performance of individual researchers during the past year
of VRP bug reports as well as previous bugSWAT event performance.

Remember, this is about _quality_, not quantity – the higher the impact of your
reports is, the more you'll be rewarded, and the higher you'll move up in the
ranking of impactful researchers, increasing your chances of receiving an
invitation to a future edition of bugSWAT.

## bugSWAT Seoul 2026

In April 2026, we invited 20 of our top bug hunters to **2 weeks of virtual**
**training** followed by a trip to Seoul, South Korea for **2 in-person days of**
**training, knowledge-sharing, and hacking** on released and unreleased AI
products – resulting in 107 discovered bugs and a reward total of **$425,111**
to date. Witnessing the creative exploits and high-level skills on display at
the Seoul 2026 event was absolutely awesome and inspiring, and once again
underlined the importance and value of bugSWAT for the security of Google
products.
