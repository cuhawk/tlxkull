If you're new to XSS, we highly recommend checking out our [interactive labs](https://portswigger.net/web-security/cross-site-scripting). They're a great introduction to the topic - covering different vulnerability varieties and how to both catch and prevent them.

Looking for something a bit more advanced? PortSwigger Research has a wide variety of cutting-edge posts covering XSS.

Hone your techniques and pick up some advanced skills with our full list of XSS research below. Covering everything from [XSS in hidden input fields](https://portswigger.net/research/xss-in-hidden-input-fields) to performing [CSS powered timing attacks by abusing Jquery](https://portswigger.net/research/abusing-jquery-for-css-powered-timing-attacks).

Make sure to get acquainted with our [cheat sheet](https://portswigger.net/research/one-xss-cheatsheet-to-rule-them-all) too - it's consistently updated with new vectors from us, as well as some insightful community contributions.
