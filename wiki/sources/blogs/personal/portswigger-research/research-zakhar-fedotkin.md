Zakhar Fedotkin is a security researcher at PortSwigger, with a deep passion for source code analysis and designing innovative methods to compromise applications. He is known for his work in exploiting vulnerabilities in image processing libraries and HTTP clients and frameworks.

He is fascinated by uncovering the reasons behind vulnerabilities and understanding why code behaves in specific ways. Zakhar likes to present his findings publicly and share his knowledge with the community. In his spare time, he loves running and reading books.
