The nominations are in for the [Top 10 Web Hacking Techniques of 2017](https://portswigger.net/blog/top-10-web-hacking-techniques-of-2017-nominations-open), so it's time to start the community vote. We're inviting everyone to select their personal top 10, and the votes will be used to build a shortlist of 15 techniques for the final selection stage.

So, go ahead and browse through the candidates, [load up the voting form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdmDzUvdD8Ng1WDUcwZ1aVZez9vDPUI9Z99kaOV7_XL4518Hg/viewform?usp=sf_link), and place your vote!
