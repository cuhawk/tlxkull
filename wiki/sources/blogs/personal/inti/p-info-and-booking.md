# Info & Booking

Inti De Ceukelaire may be available for a speaking engagement at your event or cybersecurity awareness training.

# Available live hacking shows

We offer live hacking experiences aimed towards a broad audience. All sessions come with the opportunity to hold a Q&A up to 30 minutes after the performance, included in the price and are ideal for raising security awareness in an engaging and entertaining way.

### 1. Magical Hacks

**Languages**: NL, EN

**Audience**: everyone (technical and non-technical)

**Duration:** 60 min - 90 min

**Description**: An interactive show packed with digital spectacle and real ethical hacks, presented as mind-blowing magic tricks.

Prepare to be amazed as we reveal hacks that seem impossible—until we break them down and show you how they work. Each **magical hack** comes with practical tips to help you stay cyber-savvy and protect yourself online.

With a perfect mix of entertainment, education, and humor, this show is designed for everyone—no technical background needed.

This isn’t just another cybersecurity talk. It’s a thrilling, eye-opening experience where **real hacks** unfold before your eyes!

### 2. Artifhacking Intelligence

**Languages**: NL, EN

**Audience**: everyone (technical and non-technical)

**Duration:** 60 min - 90 min

**Description**: Think AI systems are unbreakable? Think again. In this eye-opening session, I’ll take you behind the scenes and show you exactly how *anyone* can hack AI. Through live, self-crafted demos, you’ll learn how to beat the (artificial) odds, anticipate AI behavior, and subtly influence outcomes in your favor.

This hands-on experience not only teaches you how to ethically manipulate AI algorithms, but also how to detect and defend against such tactics in the real world.

Blending cutting-edge technology with practical psychology, this session offers valuable insights for professionals across IT, HR, marketing, and business leadership.
