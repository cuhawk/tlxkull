# Why subscribe?

By subscribing, you're making a direct contribution to high-quality and independent research. We're committed to producing informative, thought-provoking, and unbiased content, and your support helps us continue creating in-depth research, interviews, and analysis that inform and empower our readers.

Your subscription also helps us to prepare for potential legal threats. Despite legal protections, ethical hacking is still stigmatized, and companies may try to undermine our work. By supporting us, you enable us to be ready for any legal challenges that may arise and to continue producing high-quality, independent research.

In addition, we value the input of our subscribers and strive to provide content that is personalized and engaging. By subscribing, you'll become a part of a community that promotes free and open discourse and values diverse perspectives and ideas.

Your pledge of support helps us continue producing high-quality and independent research that informs, educates, and inspires our readers. You'll also be joining a community of like-minded individuals who value critical thinking and open dialogue. Join us today to help us further our mission of promoting independent research, and contribute to the global state of cybersecurity.
