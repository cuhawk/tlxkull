I have a bad habit of using double hyphens as dashes. I am stopping that immediately.

Hyphens are for joining words together.

There were thirty-five of them out there.

A dash is generally for a pause, and is often used as a replacement for parenthesis.

I doubt I’d be going–even with the extra incentive–because the pay wasn’t good enough.

Ampersand – mdash – semicolon, in HTML
