## AI Summary

A 0-day Cross Origin Request Forgery vulnerability was discovered in Grafana 8.x hosted on Aiven platforms, allowing an attacker to mount a CSRF attack against authenticated victims of other Grafana instances hosted on `*.aivencloud.com`. The vulnerability was caused by Grafana setting their auth cookie `grafana_session` to `SameSite=Lax; Secure`. No official remediation has been released yet.
