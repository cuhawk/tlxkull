## AI Summary

Scoped apptokens in Nextcloud could be changed by the same apptoken, allowing an attacker to gain full access to all user data. This vulnerability could be exploited by iterating through the IDs of apptokens and sending a PUT request to change the scope, even if the apptoken was not allowed to access the filesystem.
