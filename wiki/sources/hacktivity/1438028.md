## AI Summary

A vulnerability in Brave iOS versions 1.32.3 and higher allowed for XSS attacks on the privileged origin `internal://local` through the combination of two weaknesses. The first weakness was the exposure of `uuidKey` through the REFERER header due to the lack of referrer header protection in the `ReaderViewLoading.html` template. The second weakness was the lack of URL validation in the `SessionRestoreHandler`, which allowed for the execution of JavaScript code on the `internal:` domain. An attacker could exploit these vulnerabilities to elevate privileges to the `internal:` origin.
