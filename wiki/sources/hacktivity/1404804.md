## AI Summary

A XSS vulnerability was found in the email templates of Judge.me, a product review and Q&A platform for Shopify stores. The vulnerability was caused by a custom `onIgnoreTag` function in the `js-xss` library used to prevent XSS on email templates previews. An attacker could bypass this filter and achieve account takeover in 1-click by sending a malicious email template and using HMAC authentication to force another user to preview it. The vulnerability also allowed for private content leak and impersonation on support chat.
