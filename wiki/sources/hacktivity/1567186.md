## AI Summary

A vulnerability was discovered in Reddit's "Sign in with Apple" feature that allowed attackers to hijack user accounts. The vulnerability was due to a response-type switch and leaking href to XSS on www.redditmedia.com. Attackers could steal OAuth codes and access-tokens using postMessage, allowing for a full account hijack of the account in Reddit. The vulnerability was mitigated by removing the fragment part when location is sent to www.redditmedia.com and restricting the redirect_uri of Apple-ID to something that does not load a domain that could run arbitrary javascript.
