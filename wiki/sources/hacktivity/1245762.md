## AI Summary

A vulnerability was discovered in the Zenly API's SMS authentication flow, where an attacker could obtain the same session token as a legitimate user by abusing the `/SessionCreate` endpoint. Once the legitimate user validated the session with an SMS code, the attacker could also gain access to the user's account and information.
