## AI Summary

A CSRF vulnerability in the /signup/email API endpoint at khanacademy.org allowed takeovers of accounts with unconfirmed email addresses. The endpoint insufficiently verified requests came from the authenticated user. An attacker could exploit this to associate accounts with their email address and then reset the password. The attack required the target user to visit an attacker website while logged in and have an unconfirmed email address.
