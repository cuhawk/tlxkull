## AI Summary

A DOM XSS vulnerability was found on widgets.wp.com allowing injection of scripts into the DOM. This was combined with a vulnerability in the Jetpack WordPress plugin where postMessages from widgets.wp.com were used to populate avatar URLs without validation, leading to DOM XSS on WordPress sites using the Likes feature. Over 100,000 websites were affected. Input validation and output encoding was recommended to mitigate the vulnerabilities.
