## AI Summary

The Rocket.Chat vulnerability allowed arbitrary script execution in the receiving frontend client through the rendering of messages of various MessageTypes. The vulnerability affected versions 3.18.2 and 4.0.3. The issue was caused by the lack of sanitization of message parameters rendered from MessageType "render" or "data" functions.
