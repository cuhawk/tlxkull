An open redirect vulnerability was discovered in Brave's QR code scanner, which allowed attackers to direct users to malicious sites without their consent or knowledge. This vulnerability put the security of Brave users at risk and allowed them to be exposed to phishing and malware attacks. The vulnerability was present in Brave 1.50.114, Chromium 112.0.5615.49 on Android 11; Build/RP1A.200720.011.
