## AI Summary

A vulnerability was discovered that exposed personally identifiable information (PII) of tens of thousands of users and supervisors. The vulnerability was found in a system that allowed users to submit a SAAR. By modifying a URL parameter, users could view other users' SAARs, which contained sensitive information such as addresses, full names, emails, phone numbers, dates of birth, supervisor information, DODID numbers, clearance levels, and user IDs.
