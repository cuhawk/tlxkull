## AI Summary

A bug in Cloudflare's Email Routing feature allowed for the hijacking of emails sent to any domain that used Cloudflare Email Forwarding. The vulnerability allowed for the redirection of email messages for an unverified zone to a different mailbox and the overwriting of the email forwarding configuration created by the zone owner. The issue has since been fixed by the Engineering team.
