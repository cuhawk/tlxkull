## AI Summary

A blind stored cross-site scripting vulnerability was discovered due to an insecure contact form on a military website. The form did not properly sanitize user input, allowing for the insertion and submission of dangerous characters. An attacker could submit a blind XSS payload through the form, triggering XSS in the admin panel and revealing critical information such as the administrator's IP address, backend application service, titles of mail chimp customer and internal subscription emails, and admin session cookies.
