## AI Summary

The `IPS\cms\modules\front\pages\_builder::previewBlock()` method in IPS Community Suite allowed arbitrary content to be passed to the `IPS\_Theme::runProcessFunction()` method, which could be exploited to inject and execute arbitrary PHP code. This vulnerability required an account with permission to manage the sidebar and the "cms" application to be enabled.
