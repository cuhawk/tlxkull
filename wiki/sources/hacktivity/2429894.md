## AI Summary

The vulnerability in the libuv library was caused by the improper truncation of hostnames to 256 characters before calling the getaddrinfo function. This behavior allowed the creation of addresses like 0x00007f000001, which were considered valid by getaddrinfo, potentially leading to SSRF attacks and internal API access.
