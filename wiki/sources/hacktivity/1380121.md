## AI Summary

A weak Flask session key was discovered in jarvis-new.urbanclap.com, allowing attackers to modify the session state and log in as any user, resulting in a full compromise of the instance. The vulnerability was fixed by changing the REDASH_COOKIE_SECRET and REDASH_SECRET_KEY to a random value.
