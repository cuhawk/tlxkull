## AI Summary

A hidden admin account could be created by an admin in an ads creating and managing application for Reddit, allowing them to perform administrative actions on the organization, such as removing or inviting users. The malicious account information could not be seen in the members section, and even the owner of the organization could not detect or remove the account. This was possible due to a flaw in the application's role management functionalities, which allowed a user with the same email to be invited to the same organization multiple times if assigned different roles.
