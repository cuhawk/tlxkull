## AI Summary

A Universal XSS vulnerability was discovered in Brave iOS versions 1.32.3 and higher. The vulnerability was caused by three weaknesses, including the exposure of UserScriptManager.securityToken and UserScriptManager.messageHandlerToken, as well as a UXSS vulnerability in PlaylistHelper through nodeTag. An attacker could exploit these weaknesses to execute unintended code on the mainframe of a WebView, resulting in Universal XSS on arbitrary domains.
