## AI Summary

A vulnerability in GitLab allowed for a CSP-bypass XSS attack in the project settings page. The vulnerability was due to unsanitized deployment titles, which could be controlled by users and could contain arbitrary HTML content. This allowed for the execution of malicious scripts, despite the presence of a Content Security Policy (CSP) with 'strict-dynamic' value. The impact of the vulnerability was that attackers could perform arbitrary malicious requests on behalf of victims on HTTP client side.
