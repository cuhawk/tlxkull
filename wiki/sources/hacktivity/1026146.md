## AI Summary

Due to a lack of access control, an anonymous attacker could compromise the administrator account on the Questionmark Perception system, allowing them to view all fields of the questions, results, and personal information of the participants. The vulnerability was exploited by using publicly accessible service descriptions and bypassing access controls. The suggested mitigation actions include removing the service description and reconfiguring the system to deny all requests without the SOAP "Trust" header.
