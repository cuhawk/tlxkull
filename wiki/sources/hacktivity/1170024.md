## AI Summary

A vulnerability was discovered in Nextcloud that allowed an attacker to obtain write access to any federated share or public link. By obtaining a read-only public link and creating a federated share to their own instance, the attacker could accept the share and check the `oc_share_external` table to find the remote id and token. With this information, the attacker could craft a request to the victim's server to gain READ+WRITE+UPDATE+CREATE+SHARE access to the federated share or public link. This could compromise the integrity of all files in public links and federated shares.
