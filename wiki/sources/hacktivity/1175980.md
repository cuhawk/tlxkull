## AI Summary

An improper access control vulnerability was found in the Transportation Management Services Solution (TMSS) 2.0 website, which allowed unauthenticated users to access an endpoint that exposed the personal data of all registered users, including email addresses, phone numbers, full names, and secret questions. The vulnerability was caused by an incremental ID parameter that could be easily brute-forced to leak all user information.
