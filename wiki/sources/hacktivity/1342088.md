## AI Summary

A vulnerability was discovered in Flickr's login functionality, which uses Amazon Cognito. Attackers could bypass Flickr's restriction on changing registered email addresses by communicating directly with the Amazon Cognito User Pool API. This allowed attackers to take over a user's account without any user interaction, simply by linking a crafted email address to the victim's account. Flickr did not check whether the email address was verified on login, and normalized the email address received from AWS Cognito, making collisions possible.
