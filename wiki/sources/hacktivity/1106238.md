## AI Summary

A vulnerability was discovered in Mermaid, a tool used by GitLab to generate diagrams and flowcharts from text. The vulnerability allowed attackers to set the `__proto__` attribute in the directive, leading to denial of service (DoS) via prototype pollution. This could be exploited by attackers to break the page and prevent users from performing actions such as adding or editing comments.
