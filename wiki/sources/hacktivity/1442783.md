## AI Summary

An improper authentication vulnerability was discovered in the Rockstar Games Launcher. This vulnerability allowed attackers who had already gained access to a victim's Steam or Epic Games account to also gain access to the victim's Social Club account. By exploiting the "Switch Account" feature when launching a game, the attacker could take over the victim's Social Club profile, potentially leading to account theft and abuse. The issue was resolved by implementing an additional authentication token to verify user sign-ins.
