## AI Summary

A stored XSS vulnerability was discovered in GitLab.com that allowed an attacker to inject HTML in any note, issue description, or wiki page by abusing syntax_highlight_filter.rb. The vulnerability was caused by the lack of proper input sanitization. The attacker could inject a script tag by using the base tag and loading the script from their own domain, bypassing the CSP. This could lead to the creation of tokens and take over of SSO accounts.
