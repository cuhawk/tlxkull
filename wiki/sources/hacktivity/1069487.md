## AI Summary

The Node.js debugger (--inspect option) was vulnerable to DNS rebinding attacks due to an excessive whitelist that included "localhost6". An attacker could bypass the protection by controlling the victim's DNS server or spoofing its responses, allowing them to gain access to the debugger and potentially execute remote code.
