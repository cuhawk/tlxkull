## AI Summary

A vulnerability was found in a Social Club feature that allowed users to import their friends list from social media sites. An attacker could exploit this feature by tricking a victim into visiting a malicious site containing a specific script, which would link the victim's Social Club account to the attacker's Facebook account via CSRF. This would give the attacker control over the victim's Social Club account. The issue has been resolved.
