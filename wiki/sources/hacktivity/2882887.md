## AI Summary

CVE-2024-53908: Django potential SQL injection in `HasKey(lhs, rhs)` on Oracle was reported. The vulnerability was found in the direct usage of the `django.db.models.fields.json.HasKey` lookup on Oracle databases when untrusted data was used as an `lhs` value. Applications that used the `jsonfield.has_key` lookup through the `__` syntax were unaffected.
