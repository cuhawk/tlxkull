## AI Summary

A vulnerability existed in the Basecamp 3 Android app that allowed an attacker to steal a user's authentication tokens by forcing them to enter the app through a specially crafted deep link. The vulnerability was due to a check that could be bypassed by using a specific URL format with an attacker-controlled "proceed_to" parameter. Once exploited, the attacker could retrieve the user's authentication tokens without physical access to the device.
