A vulnerability allowed an attacker to inject HTML code into emails sent from a shared dashboard on a platform. The attacker could craft a believable spearphishing email coming from an email address owned by the platform. The vulnerability was due to the message not being fully sanitized for HTML characters before sending to the recipient.
