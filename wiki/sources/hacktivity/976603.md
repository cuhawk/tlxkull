## AI Summary

A vulnerability was discovered that allowed for a denial of service attack on any organization's SSO integration and the potential for account takeovers. The vulnerability was due to the ability to create an `entityId` identical to that of another organization except with a space at the end, which would prioritize the new `entityId` and allow for unauthorized access. The vulnerability was fixed before SSO became available to Grammarly customers.
