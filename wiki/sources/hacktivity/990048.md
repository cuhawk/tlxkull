## AI Summary

Improper validation at the partners login allowed an attacker to get the OTP of the victim on both the attacker's number and the victim's number. The vulnerability was due to the lack of strict checks on the `country_isd` parameter. The backend generated the OTP for the `phone_number` parameter but appended the `country_isd` parameter to the `phone_number` parameter before sending the SMS without any major checks.
