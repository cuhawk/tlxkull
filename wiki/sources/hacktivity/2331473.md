## AI Summary

The report described an Open Redirect vulnerability in the vcc-*.8x8.com/AGUI/█.php endpoint, where a filter that prevented the use of [1-9] and [a-z] characters in the `subdomain` parameter could be bypassed by utilizing a non-Latin domain. The vulnerability was demonstrated by redirecting to the `नमस्ते.भारत` domain.
