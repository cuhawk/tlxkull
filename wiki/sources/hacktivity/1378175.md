## AI Summary

An ingress-nginx annotation injection vulnerability allowed an attacker with permission to create/modify ingresses in one namespace to inject content into the connection-proxy-header annotation and read arbitrary files from the ingress controller (including the service account), which had permission to read secrets in all namespaces. The vulnerability was caused by most template variables not being escaped properly in `nginx.tmpl`, leading to injection of arbitrary nginx directives.
